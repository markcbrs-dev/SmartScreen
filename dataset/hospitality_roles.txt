Job Title: Barista
Company: Keystone Manpower Corporation
Location: Unit 306, 3rd floor, 160 West Ave, Quezon City, 1105, Metro Manila
Employment Type: Full-Time or Part-Time
Reporting To: Area Supervisor

Job Description

Duties and Responsibilities:

- Comply with health and safety regulations.
- Communicate customer feedback to managers and recommend new menu items.

Bartender
Company: Keystone Manpower Corporation
Location: Unit 306, 3rd floor, 160 West Ave, Quezon City, 1105 Metro Manila
Employment Type: Full-Time or Part-Time
Reporting to: Area Supervisor

Job Description

Duties and Responsibilities:
- Responsible to prepare and serve drinks to customers.
- Mix and match ingredients in order to create classic and innovative drinks in accordance with customer needs and expectations.
- Interact with the hotel guest and ensure they have a mixing and serving beverages to guests and serves in a friendly and efficient manner.
- Interacts with customers, take orders for drinks and snacks.
- Present bar menu.
- Serve snacks and drinks to the customer.
- Check identification of the guest to make sure they meet age requirements for the purchase of alcohol and tobacco products.
- Mix ingredients to prepare cocktails and other drinks.
- Mix drinks, cocktails and other bar beverages as ordered and in compliance with hotels standard drink recipes.
- Prepare alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages.
- Service wine and beer to guest.
- Arrange bottles and glasses to make attractive displays.
- Assess customers needs and preferences and make recommendations.
- Sell or influence others for up selling and suggestive selling.
- Provide recommendations and suggestions to guest for choosing drinks and snacks.
- Serve customer in a friendly and helpful manner.
- Keep the bar counter and work area neat and clean at all times.
- Determine when a customer has had too much alcohol and it required refusing any further serving on a polite way.
- Demonstrate a thorough knowledge of food and beverage products, menus and promotions.
- Handle and move objects such as glasses/bottles using hands and arms.
- Clean up after customer and clean work area.
- Clear ashtray as when required.
- Wash glassware and utensils after each use.
- Maintain a clean working area by sweeping, vacuuming, dusting and cleaning of glass doors and windows, etc., if required.
- Perform physical activities such as lifting and stooping.
- Maintain liquor inventory and consumption.
- Collect payment for drinks served and balance all receipts.
- Prepare inventory or purchase requisitions as needed to replenish supplies.
- Ensure that the assigned bar area is fully equipped with tools and products needed for mixing beverages and serving guests.
- Stay guest focused and nurture an excellent guest experience.
- Comply with all food and beverages regulation.
- Respond properly to any hotel emergency or safety situation.
- Perform other duties as and when assigned by the F&B Manager or his/her delegates.
