Position Overview
We are seeking a dedicated and innovative Computer Science Professor to join our faculty. The successful candidate will be responsible for teaching undergraduate and/or graduate courses, conducting impactful research, mentoring students, and contributing to the academic community through service and leadership.

Key Responsibilities:
Teaching & Curriculum Development
- Deliver engaging lectures, labs, and seminars in core and specialized areas of computer science (e.g., algorithms, AI, software engineering, cybersecurity).
- Develop and update course materials to reflect current industry practices and emerging technologies.
- Supervise student projects, theses, and dissertations.

Research & Scholarship
- Conduct original research in computer science and publish in peer-reviewed journals and conferences.
- Pursue external funding opportunities to support research initiatives.
- Collaborate with colleagues across disciplines to advance innovation.

Student Mentorship
- Advise and mentor students on academic, career, and research pathways.
- Foster an inclusive and supportive learning environment.

Service & Leadership
- Participate in departmental committees, curriculum planning, and accreditation processes.
- Contribute to outreach activities, industry partnerships, and community engagement.

Qualifications
- Education: Ph.D. in Computer Science or a closely related field (required).
- Experience: Prior teaching experience at the university level preferred; strong research record with publications in recognized venues.

Skills:
- Expertise in one or more areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, or Systems.
- Strong communication and presentation skills.
- Ability to integrate technology and innovative pedagogy into teaching.

Preferred Attributes
- Demonstrated commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion in academia.
- Experience in interdisciplinary collaboration.
- Industry experience or applied research background is a plus.
